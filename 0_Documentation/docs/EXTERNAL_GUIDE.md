# GCC Database Solution - External User Guide

## Overview
This guide is for External users who have limited access to the GCC Database Solution. External users can view certificates associated with their organization and download relevant documentation, but cannot create or modify certificate information.

## Login and Access
- **URL**: http://localhost:5101/Account/Login
- **Default Credentials**: partner@amazon.com / Partner123!
- **Role**: External (limited view access)

## Available Features

### View Certificates
External users can access certificates that are specifically assigned to their organization or partnership.

#### Browse Certificate Tiles
1. After login, you'll see the certificate tile view
2. Certificates are automatically filtered to show only those relevant to your organization
3. Use the infinite scroll to browse through available certificates
4. Click on any certificate tile to view detailed information

#### Search for Specific Certificates
1. Use the search bar to find specific certificates
2. Search by:
   - Certificate number
   - Product name or description
   - Product IDs (ASIN, UPC, GTIN, etc.)
3. Results are automatically filtered to your accessible certificates
4. Use autocomplete suggestions for faster searching

### Certificate Details
Access comprehensive information about certificates relevant to your organization.

#### View Certificate Information
1. Click on a certificate tile to open details
2. Review certificate information:
   - Certificate number and validity dates
   - Product information and specifications
   - Certification body details
   - Associated product IDs
3. View certification standards and compliance information
4. Check certificate status and expiration dates

#### Download Certificate Documents
1. From the certificate details page
2. Click "Download PDF" to get the official certificate
3. Save certificate for your records
4. Print or share as needed for compliance purposes

### Product ID Information
Access product identifier information for your products.

#### View Associated Product IDs
1. Review all product IDs associated with certificates
2. Product types available:
   - **ASINs**: Amazon Standard Identification Numbers
   - **UPCs**: Universal Product Codes
   - **GTINs**: Global Trade Item Numbers
   - **EANs**: European Article Numbers
   - **SKUs**: Stock Keeping Units
   - **MPNs**: Manufacturer Part Numbers
3. Use this information for inventory and compliance tracking

### Export Capabilities
Limited export functionality for your accessible data.

#### Export Certificate Data
1. Select certificates you want to export
2. Choose export format:
   - **CSV**: For data analysis and inventory systems
   - **Excel**: For reporting and documentation
3. Export includes only certificates you have access to
4. Use exported data for:
   - Compliance reporting
   - Internal inventory tracking
   - Audit documentation

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Cannot See Expected Certificates
1. Verify you're logged in with correct account
2. Check that certificates are assigned to your organization
3. Contact administrator to verify access permissions
4. Clear browser cache and try again

#### Search Not Working
1. Try broader search terms
2. Check spelling of product names or numbers
3. Verify you have access to the certificates you're searching for
4. Use different search criteria

#### Download Problems
1. Ensure pop-up blockers are disabled
2. Check browser download permissions
3. Verify file isn't blocked by corporate firewall
4. Try different browser if issues persist
