# GCC Database Solution - Public User Guide

## Overview
Welcome to the GCC Database Solution public access portal. This guide helps public users search and view certified product information without requiring an account or login.

## Accessing the System
- **URL**: http://localhost:5101/Certificate/CertificateTileView
- **No Login Required**: Public access available immediately
- **Features**: Search certificates, view basic information, download public certificates

## Available Features

### Certificate Search
Search through publicly available certificates and product information.

#### Basic Search
1. Enter search terms in the main search bar
2. Search by:
   - Product name or description
   - Company name
   - Certificate number (if known)
   - Product categories
3. Use autocomplete suggestions for faster results
4. Results appear instantly as you type

#### Advanced Filtering
1. Use category filters to narrow results:
   - Product type (electronics, textiles, etc.)
   - Certification standard
   - Company or manufacturer
2. Apply date filters for recent certifications
3. Combine multiple filters for precise results

### Browse Certificates
Explore available certificates through the interactive tile interface.

#### Certificate Tiles
1. Scroll through certificate tiles on the main page
2. Each tile shows:
   - Product name and image (if available)
   - Company name
   - Certificate validity status
   - Key product identifiers
3. Click any tile to view detailed information

#### Infinite Scroll
- New certificates load automatically as you scroll
- No pagination - continuous browsing experience
- Smooth performance with large certificate databases

### View Certificate Details
Access detailed information about specific certificates.

#### Certificate Information
1. Click on any certificate tile
2. View comprehensive details:
   - Full certificate information
   - Product specifications
   - Certification standards met
   - Validity dates and status
   - Certifying organization details

#### Product Identifiers
Review product identification information:
- **UPCs**: Universal Product Codes for retail
- **GTINs**: Global Trade Item Numbers
- **EANs**: European Article Numbers
- **Model Numbers**: Manufacturer product codes
- **Brand Information**: Product branding details

### Download Public Documents
Access publicly available certificate documentation.

#### Available Downloads
1. Public certificate summaries
2. Product compliance statements
3. Certification mark usage guidelines
4. Basic product information sheets

#### Download Process
1. Navigate to certificate details page
2. Look for "Public Downloads" section
3. Click on available document links
4. Save documents for your reference

## Search Tips and Best Practices

### Effective Searching
- **Use Specific Terms**: Product names, model numbers work best
- **Try Variations**: Different spellings or abbreviations
- **Use Quotes**: For exact phrase matching ("product name")
- **Combine Terms**: Multiple keywords for better results

### Category Navigation
- Browse by product categories for exploration
- Use industry-specific terms for targeted results
- Filter by certification types for compliance research
- Sort by date for newest certifications

### Understanding Results
- **Green Status**: Current valid certification
- **Yellow Status**: Expiring soon (within 30 days)
- **Red Status**: Expired certification
- **Certification Marks**: Indicate specific standards met

## Understanding Certification Information

### Certificate Status
- **Active**: Currently valid certification
- **Expiring**: Valid but approaching expiration
- **Expired**: No longer valid certification
- **Suspended**: Temporarily invalid

### Certification Types
- **Environmental**: Eco-friendly and sustainable products
- **Safety**: Product safety and quality standards
- **Performance**: Product performance specifications
- **Compliance**: Regulatory compliance certifications

### Standards Organizations
Common certifying bodies you'll encounter:

- Regional regulatory authorities

## Limitations of Public Access

### Information Restrictions
- Basic certificate information only
- No access to detailed technical reports
- Limited historical information
- No proprietary or confidential data

### Functionality Limitations
- View-only access (no downloading of full documents)
- Cannot access internal product IDs
- No advanced reporting features
- No contact information for certified companies

### Data Currency
- Information updated regularly but may not be real-time
- Some certificates may have pending status updates
- Recent changes may not be immediately reflected
- Contact certificate holders for most current information
