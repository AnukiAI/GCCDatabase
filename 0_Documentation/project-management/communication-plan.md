## Issue and Escalation Communication

### Issue Classification and Communication

#### Critical Issues (Project-stopping)
- **Response Time**: Immediate (within 2 hours)
- **Communication Method**: Phone call + email follow-up
- **Audience**: PointSolve Management, GreenCircle Executive Team
- **Follow-up**: Daily updates until resolved

#### High Priority Issues (Major impact)
- **Response Time**: Same day (within 4 hours)
- **Communication Method**: Email with action plan
- **Audience**: Relevant stakeholders based on issue type
- **Follow-up**: Every 2 days until resolved

#### Medium Priority Issues (Moderate impact)
- **Response Time**: Next business day
- **Communication Method**: Email or regular status update
- **Audience**: Relevant stakeholders
- **Follow-up**: Weekly updates

#### Low Priority Issues (Minor impact)
- **Response Time**: Within 1 week
- **Communication Method**: Regular status update
- **Audience**: Technical stakeholders
- **Follow-up**: Monthly review

### Escalation Path

1. **Level 1**: Stephen West (Project Manager) - All issues initially
2. **Level 2**: PointSolve Management - Critical issues, budget impacts, resource needs
3. **Level 3**: GreenCircle Executive Team - Client relationship issues, major scope changes
4. **Level 4**: External expertise - Specialized technical or legal issues

## Change Communication Process

### Change Request Communication
1. **Change Identification**: Document change request with impact assessment
2. **Stakeholder Notification**: Inform all affected stakeholders within 24 hours
3. **Change Review**: Schedule review meeting with decision-makers
4. **Decision Communication**: Communicate approval/rejection and reasoning
5. **Implementation Updates**: Regular progress updates on approved changes

### Change Communication Template
- **Change Description**: What is changing and why
- **Impact Assessment**: Timeline, budget, resource, and quality impacts
- **Stakeholder Impact**: Who is affected and how
- **Decision Required**: What needs to be decided and by whom
- **Timeline**: When decision needed and implementation timeline

## Emergency Communication Procedures

### Emergency Communication Triggers
- **System security breach or data compromise**
- **Complete system failure affecting public access**
- **Critical project timeline impacts (>2 week delays)**
- **Major stakeholder concerns or escalations**

### Emergency Communication Protocol
1. **Immediate Assessment**: Evaluate situation and impact within 1 hour
2. **Stakeholder Notification**: Contact critical stakeholders within 2 hours
3. **Status Updates**: Provide updates every 4 hours until resolved
4. **Resolution Communication**: Final status and lessons learned within 24 hours of resolution
