● TextBelt SMS Service Overview

  TextBelt is a simple, developer-friendly SMS API designed as a budget alternative to premium services like Twilio:

  📱 What is TextBelt?

  Core Concept:
  - "Keep it Simple" SMS API for developers who just want to send SMS
  - No account setup required for basic usage
  - No recurring billing - pay only for what you use
  - Open-source roots with commercial reliability

  💰 Pricing Structure

  Free Tier:
  - 1 free SMS per day using key=textbelt
  - Perfect for testing and light development

  Paid Tiers:
  - Pay-as-you-go model without recurring fees
  - Purchase credits/quota as needed
  - Much cheaper than Twilio (exact pricing varies)
  - Quota expires only after 365 days of inactivity

  Cost Comparison:
  - Twilio: ~$0.0075 per SMS + monthly fees
  - TextBelt: Significantly lower per-message cost, no monthly fees

  🔧 Technical Features

  API Simplicity:
  curl -X POST https://textbelt.com/text \
    --data-urlencode phone='5555555555' \
    --data-urlencode message='Hello world' \
    -d key=textbelt

  Supported Features:
  - ✅ Send SMS to 221 countries worldwide
  - ✅ Delivery status tracking (DELIVERED, SENT, FAILED)
  - ✅ Receive SMS replies (US numbers only)
  - ✅ Quota management - check remaining credits
  - ✅ Multi-language SDKs (Python, Node.js, PHP, C#, etc.)

  🌍 Coverage & Reliability

  Geographic Coverage:
  - 221 countries worldwide
  - US domestic and international SMS
  - Reply handling for US numbers

  Reliability:
  - Commercial-grade infrastructure (different from open-source version)
  - Reliable carrier partnerships for delivery
  - Rate limiting: Max 1 SMS per second
