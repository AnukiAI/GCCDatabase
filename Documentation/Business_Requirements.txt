Title: Digital Certificate Management Platform
Description: The organization SHALL implement a centralized digital platform to manage, track, and share product certification information across multiple stakeholders.

Business Objective:
- Replace manual certificate tracking processes
- Reduce certificate search time by 80%
- Improve compliance verification efficiency
- Enable real-time certificate status monitoring
- Facilitate partner and client access to certificates

Success Criteria:
- All certificates digitized within 6 months
- 90% user adoption rate within 3 months
- 50% reduction in compliance audit time
