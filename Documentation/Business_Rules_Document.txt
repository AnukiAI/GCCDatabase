                        CERTIFICATE BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE BR-CERT-001: Certificate Number Uniqueness
Description: Certificate numbers must be unique across the entire system
Implementation:
- Check for duplicates before creation
- Case-insensitive comparison
- No special characters allowed except dash and underscore
- Maximum length: 50 characters
- Minimum length: 5 characters
Exception Handling: Display error "Certificate number already exists"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-002: Certificate Validity Period
Description: Certificates have specific validity period constraints
Implementation:
- Maximum validity period: 5 years
- Minimum validity period: 1 day
- Expiration date must be after effective date
- Effective date cannot be more than 30 days in future
- Warning if validity period exceeds 3 years
Exception Handling: Display appropriate validation message

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-003: Certificate Status Transitions
Description: Certificate status changes must follow allowed transitions
Allowed Transitions:
- Draft → Pending → Active
- Active → Expired (automatic on expiration date)
- Active → Suspended → Active
- Active → Revoked (final state)
- Suspended → Revoked (final state)
- Pending → Rejected → Draft
Restrictions:
- Cannot transition from Revoked to any state
- Cannot transition from Expired to Active without renewal
- Only admins can transition to Revoked

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-004: Certificate Auto-Numbering Format
Description: System generates certificate numbers automatically
Format Options:
- Pattern 1: {PREFIX}-{YEAR}-{SEQUENCE} (e.g., CERT-2025-00001)
- Pattern 2: {CATEGORY}-{YEAR}{MONTH}-{SEQUENCE} (e.g., FOOD-202501-001)
- Pattern 3: {COMPANY}-{TYPE}-{SEQUENCE} (e.g., ACME-GMP-1234)
Rules:
- Sequence resets yearly if year is in pattern
- Sequence continues if no year in pattern
- Manual override allowed for admins only
- Reserved ranges can be configured

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-005: Public Visibility Rules
Description: Determines when certificates are publicly visible
Conditions for Public Visibility:
- Status = Active AND
- Visibility = Public AND
- Effective Date <= Today AND
- Expiration Date > Today AND
- Company.IsPublic = true
Exceptions:
- Admin can override visibility
- Confidential certificates never public
- Draft/Pending never public

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-006: Mandatory Fields by Certificate Type
Description: Different certificate types require different fields
GMP Certificates:
- Manufacturing location (required)
- Batch/Lot numbers (optional)
- Production date (required)

ISO Certificates:
- Standard version (required)
- Scope statement (required)
- Certification body (required)

Organic Certificates:
- Certifying agency (required)
- Organic percentage (required)
- Ingredient list (required)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-CERT-007: Expiry Notification Schedule
Description: Automated notifications sent before certificate expiry
Schedule:
- 90 days before: First notification
- 60 days before: Second notification
- 30 days before: Third notification (marked urgent)
- 14 days before: Weekly notifications start
- 7 days before: Daily notifications start
- Day of expiry: Final warning
- 1 day after: Expiry confirmation
Recipients:
- Certificate owner (always)
- Company admin (always)
- Designated contacts (if configured)

================================================================================
                         PRODUCT BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE BR-PROD-001: Product Code Validation
Description: Product identification codes must meet format requirements

ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number):
- Format: 10 alphanumeric characters
- Example: B08N5WRWNW
- Validation: Check with Amazon API if enabled

UPC (Universal Product Code):
- Format: 12 digits
- Check digit validation required
- Example: 012345678905

GTIN (Global Trade Item Number):
- Format: 8, 12, 13, or 14 digits
- Check digit validation required
- Must be unique per product

EAN (European Article Number):
- Format: 8 or 13 digits
- Check digit validation required
- EAN-13 preferred for new products

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-PROD-002: Product Category Hierarchy
Description: Products must follow category hierarchy rules
Structure:
- Level 1: Category (e.g., Food & Beverage)
- Level 2: Subcategory (e.g., Dairy Products)
- Level 3: Type (e.g., Cheese)
Rules:
- Product must have at least Category
- Cannot skip hierarchy levels
- Maximum 4 levels deep
- Category changes require approval

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-PROD-003: Product Name Constraints
Description: Product naming conventions and restrictions
Rules:
- Maximum length: 200 characters
- Minimum length: 3 characters
- No special characters: < > & " '
- No profanity (filtered list)
- No trademark violations
- Must be unique within company
- Case-insensitive uniqueness check

================================================================================
                          USER BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE BR-USER-001: Password Complexity Requirements
Description: Passwords must meet security standards
Requirements:
- Minimum 8 characters
- Maximum 128 characters
- At least 1 uppercase letter
- At least 1 lowercase letter
- At least 1 number
- At least 1 special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot contain username
- Cannot contain common words (top 1000)
- Cannot reuse last 5 passwords

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-USER-002: Account Lockout Policy
Description: Prevent brute force attacks through account lockout
Rules:
- Lock after 5 failed attempts within 15 minutes
- Lockout duration: 30 minutes
- Admin notification after 3 lockouts in 24 hours
- IP blocking after 10 failed attempts
- CAPTCHA required after 3 failed attempts

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-USER-003: Session Management Rules
Description: Control user session behavior
Rules:
- Session timeout: 30 minutes idle
- Absolute timeout: 8 hours
- Single session per user (configurable)
- Session extends on activity
- Warning at 5 minutes before timeout
- Secure session cookies only
- Session invalidation on password change

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-USER-004: User Role Hierarchy
Description: Define role permissions and inheritance
Hierarchy (highest to lowest):
1. System Admin (all permissions)
2. Company Admin (company-wide permissions)
3. Analyst (read/write certificates)
4. External User - Client (own company read)
5. External User - Partner (program read)
6. Public User (public certificates only)

Inheritance Rules:
- Higher roles inherit lower role permissions
- Custom roles inherit from base roles
- Permission denial overrides inheritance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-USER-005: Email Verification Requirements
Description: Email verification process and rules
Rules:
- Verification required for new accounts
- Verification link expires in 24 hours
- Maximum 3 resend requests per day
- Email change requires re-verification
- Unverified accounts deleted after 30 days
- Notification on successful verification

================================================================================
                         COMPANY BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE BR-COMP-001: Company Name Uniqueness
Description: Company names must be unique with variations considered
Rules:
- Exact match check (case-insensitive)
- Similar name warning (Levenshtein distance < 3)
- Abbreviation check (Inc, LLC, Ltd, Corp)
- Special character normalization
- DBA (Doing Business As) names tracked
- Historical names preserved

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-COMP-002: Company Verification Levels
Description: Companies have different verification levels
Levels:
1. Unverified (self-registered)
2. Email Verified (email domain confirmed)
3. Document Verified (business docs provided)
4. Fully Verified (physical verification done)

Privileges by Level:
- Unverified: View only, no certificates
- Email Verified: Can request certificates
- Document Verified: Can manage certificates
- Fully Verified: API access, bulk operations

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-COMP-003: Company Suspension Rules
Description: Conditions for automatic company suspension
Triggers:
- No active certificates for 2 years
- Payment overdue > 90 days
- Compliance violations (3 strikes)
- Security breach from company user
- Legal/regulatory action

Effects:
- All certificates marked "Company Suspended"
- User access restricted to read-only
- API access revoked
- Notifications sent to all users

RULE BR-DATA-001: Data Retention Policy
Description: How long different data types are retained
Retention Periods:
- Active certificates: Indefinite
- Expired certificates: 7 years
- Deleted certificates: 90 days (soft delete)
- Audit logs: 2 years minimum
- User activity logs: 1 year
- System logs: 90 days
- Temporary files: 24 hours
- Export files: 7 days
- Email archives: 1 year

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-DATA-002: Export Limitations
Description: Constraints on data export operations
Rules:
- Maximum 10,000 records per export
- Maximum 5 exports per hour per user
- Maximum file size: 50MB
- Formats: Excel, CSV, PDF, JSON
- Watermark on PDF exports
- Export logs retained 90 days
- Sensitive fields excluded by default

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-DATA-003: Bulk Operation Limits
Description: Constraints on bulk operations
Limits:
- Bulk create: 100 records maximum
- Bulk update: 500 records maximum
- Bulk delete: 50 records maximum
- Bulk import: 1000 rows per file
- Processing timeout: 5 minutes
- Queue limit: 3 operations per user
- Rollback on >10% failure rate

================================================================================
                        INTEGRATION BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE BR-INT-001: API Rate Limiting
Description: API usage limits per tier
Tiers:
- Public: 10 requests/minute
- Basic: 100 requests/minute
- Premium: 1000 requests/minute
- Enterprise: Custom limits

Rules:
- Burst allowance: 20% above limit
- Rate limit headers in response
- 429 status on limit exceeded
- Exponential backoff required
- Daily request cap: tier × 1440

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-INT-002: Webhook Retry Policy
Description: How failed webhooks are retried
Schedule:
- 1st retry: 1 minute
- 2nd retry: 5 minutes
- 3rd retry: 15 minutes
- 4th retry: 1 hour
- 5th retry: 6 hours
- Final retry: 24 hours

Rules:
- Stop on 2xx response
- Stop on 410 Gone
- Alert admin after 3 failures
- Disable endpoint after 7 days failing

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-INT-003: Data Synchronization Rules
Description: Rules for syncing with external systems
Rules:
- Full sync: Once daily at 2 AM
- Incremental sync: Every 15 minutes
- Conflict resolution: Latest timestamp wins
- Sync validation: Checksum comparison
- Failed sync notification: After 3 attempts
- Manual sync: Limited to 1 per hour
- Sync log retention: 30 days

                        NOTIFICATION BUSINESS RULES

RULE BR-NOTIF-001: Notification Priorities
Description: How notifications are prioritized and delivered
Priority Levels:
1. Critical: Immediate delivery, all channels
2. High: Within 5 minutes, email + in-app
3. Medium: Within 1 hour, email
4. Low: Daily digest, email

Escalation:
- Critical unread for 1 hour → SMS
- High unread for 4 hours → Escalate to manager
- Undeliverable email → In-app only

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE BR-NOTIF-002: Notification Frequency Limits
Description: Prevent notification spam
Limits:
- Maximum 10 emails per day per user
- Maximum 50 emails per day per company
- Digest mode after 5 notifications/hour
- Quiet hours: 10 PM - 6 AM (configurable)
- Vacation mode: Hold non-critical
- Unsubscribe honor period: 90 days
