Recommendation

Anuki Recommends Option B (Buffered Timeline)

  1. Historical pattern: Every UAT cycle on this project has generated 15–30 new items. Option A assumes a clean retest with zero new findings, which has not occurred in any prior cycle.
  2. Development time is identical: Both options allocate 6 weeks for SP-API implementation and 2 weeks for testing. The buffer is entirely in the UAT/stabilization phase where risk is highest.
  3. Accountability protection: A missed aggressive deadline damages the Amazon relationship more than a met conservative one.
  4. Q3 alignment: Option B completes by end of Q3 2026, a natural reporting boundary.

However, if Amazon requires the aggressive timeline, Option A is achievable provided GCC commits to completing UAT retest by May 15 with dedicated testing time and no new feature requests are introduced during the stabilization phase.
