User (n) → (n) Partner                                    ← User associated with partners

## External User Data Filtering (CLAUDE-BACKUP.md)
- External with **Companies**: WHERE Product.CompanyId IN (user's companies)
- External with **Partners**: WHERE Certificate.Partner = user's partner name
- External with **BOTH**: OR logic - matches EITHER company OR partner

Key Insight: Partner users are filtered by the Certificate.Partner field, NOT by company association. External Client users are filtered by their User.Companies association.

Partner Role

Amazon, Novi employees
WHERE Certificate.Partner = 'Amazon'
  • Partner: REQUIRED
  • Companies: NOT NEEDED
  • Sees ALL certificates tagged with their partner
  • Filter is on certificate's Partner field

External Client Role

Company representatives
WHERE Product.CompanyId IN (user's companies)
  • Companies: REQUIRED
  • Partner: OPTIONAL (for OR logic)
  • Sees only their associated companies' data
  • Filter is on user's company associations

Hybrid (Both)

External with Partner access
WHERE CompanyId IN (...) OR Partner = '...'
  • Companies: REQUIRED
  • Partner: REQUIRED
  • Sees data matching EITHER condition
  • Broader access via OR logic
