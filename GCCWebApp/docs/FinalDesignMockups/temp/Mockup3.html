GreenCircle Certification Process

Our rigorous certification process ensures that products meet strict sustainability standards

1

Application

Companies submit an application detailing their product and the certification claims they seek to validate.

2

Documentation Review

Our team reviews all supporting documentation including materials, manufacturing processes, and supply chain information.

3

On-Site Audit

GreenCircle conducts an on-site audit to verify the accuracy of claims and collect additional data.

4

Scientific Validation

Our scientific team analyzes all data to validate sustainability claims according to international standards.

5

Certification

Approved products receive GreenCircle certification and are eligible for inclusion in partner programs like Amazon's Climate Pledge Friendly.
