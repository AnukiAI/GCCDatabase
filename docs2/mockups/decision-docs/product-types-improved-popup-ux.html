Product Types 3-Level Hierarchy

Product Types use a 3-level hierarchy structure. Each level has a specific purpose and relationship to the others.

Category
e.g., Personal Care
Type
e.g., Skincare
Subcategory
e.g., Moisturizers
Level 1: Category

Broad product groupings. Examples: Personal Care, Household, Food & Beverage, Baby Products

Level 2: Type

Product types within a category. Examples: Skincare, Hair Care, Cleaning Products

Level 3: Subcategory

Specific product subcategories. Examples: Moisturizers, Shampoos, All-Purpose Cleaners

Add Category (Level 1)

Top-level product grouping - broadest classification

Add Product Category
What is a Category?
Categories are the top level of product organization. They represent broad product groupings like "Personal Care" or "Household". Each category can contain multiple Types.
Enter a broad product grouping name. This will be the top level in the product hierarchy.
Category Examples
  • Personal Care - Skincare, hair care, body care products
  • Household - Cleaning supplies, home products
  • Baby Products - Diapers, wipes, baby care items
  • Food & Beverage - Food items and drinks
  • Pet Products - Pet food, grooming, accessories
Controls display order. Lower numbers appear first.

Types are the second level of product organization. They represent product types within a category (e.g., "Skincare" under "Personal Care"). Each type can contain multiple Subcategories.

Key UX Improvements for Product Types

Context Awareness

Info hints explain each level's purpose. Context bars show parent path. Breadcrumbs display full hierarchy.

Shows existing siblings to prevent duplicates and help users understand current structure.
